# YellowBook

> **YellowBook is a private, wholesome friend notebook for real life—built to help you stay connected without becoming social media or “tracking people.”**

Most people don’t struggle to *make* friends—they struggle to *sustain* friendships when life gets busy. YellowBook is a calm place to remember people, keep gentle context, and follow through on connection in a way that feels natural—on your terms.

This README is intentionally **timeless**. It describes the product’s **vibe, goal, and boundaries**—not features, architecture, or implementation.

---

## Purpose

Help people maintain meaningful relationships with **less effort, less guilt, and more joy**.

YellowBook is designed to:

* Reduce friction around remembering, reaching out, and following through.
* Protect user agency: the user decides what matters, what to do, and when.
* Preserve emotional safety: no shame, no comparison.
* Respect privacy: no surveillance patterns, no hidden collection.
* Favor real-world connection over in-app time.

---

## North Star Experience

Using YellowBook should feel like:

1. Opening a calm notebook.
2. Seeing *your* people and context.
3. Choosing a next step—or choosing to do nothing.
4. Leaving with more clarity, less pressure, and more connection.

If a change doesn’t improve this experience, it shouldn’t ship.

---

## What YellowBook Is

* A **private tool for real social life**.
* A **memory + intention system** for friendships.
* A calm place to keep lightweight context that makes reaching out easier.

## What YellowBook Is Not

* **Social media** (no feeds, follower dynamics, or virality loops).
* **Surveillance** (no scraping, passive mining, or stealth syncing).
* **Competitive productivity** (no streaks, rankings, or “friend scores”).
* **Behavior-shaping algorithms** (no manipulation or compulsive mechanics).

---

## Product Principles

Non-negotiables:

1. **User-led by default**
   The user controls what exists, what happens next, and whether anything reminds or suggests.
2. **Private by default**
   Relationship context is personal and user-controlled.
3. **No creep factor**
   No ranking people, tracking vibes, or passive social graph behavior.
4. **Gentle, not guilt**
   Welcome users back warmly; never moralize inactivity.
5. **Wholesome over viral**
   Trust and wellbeing beat growth hacks.
6. **Small effort, real outcomes**
   Make real-world connection easier through low-friction steps.
7. **Agency and reversibility**
   Clear choices, easy undo, simple controls.
8. **Clarity over cleverness**
   Plain language, no dark patterns.
9. **Consent-aware coordination**
   If anything involves others, keep it explicit, lightweight, and respectful.

## Tone and Behavior

### Personality

Calm • Warm • Creative • Softly delightful

### Language

Prefer: **“No pressure.” “If you want.” “You’re in control.” “Keep it simple.”**
Avoid: **“Overdue.” “Missed.” “Neglected.” “Tracking.” “Friendship score.”**

### Interaction

* Quiet by default; easy exits; nothing punitive.
* Subtle celebration of kindness.
* Optimize for leaving the app to do the real thing.

---

## Privacy, Trust, and Safety

YellowBook’s promise is **wholesome and safe**—technically and emotionally.

* **Data minimization:** collect only what’s needed for user-visible utility.
* **No hidden ingestion:** no message scraping, stealth syncing, or silent graph-building.
* **Consent-driven:** sharing (if any) is explicit, scoped, and reversible.
* **Clear privacy UX:** plain language; obvious visibility; easy deletion.
* **Emotional safety:** no comparison, leaderboards, or “relationship health” scoring.

---

## Measurement Philosophy

Metrics validate that YellowBook helps people feel **more connected**—not more engaged.

**North Star (proposed):** *Meaningful outcomes per active user per week*
Meaningful outcomes reflect real-world intent and wellbeing—not taps or time-spent.

Avoid incentives that create guilt, compulsion, spammy outreach, or comparison.

---

## Contributing

Every change must pass:

* **User benefit:** What human moment does this support?
* **Agency:** Can it be silenced, opted out of, undone, and deleted?
* **Emotional risk:** Could it add guilt, pressure, or comparison?
* **Privacy:** What data is touched—can we do less?
* **Visibility/consent:** If anything is shared, is it explicit and reversible?
* **Copy:** Does it feel warm and non-judgmental?

If it doesn’t pass, redesign or drop it.

## FAQ

**Is YellowBook social media?**
No—no feeds, followers, or engagement loops.

**Does YellowBook read my private messages?**
No—everything is explicit and user-controlled.

**Is it useful if my friends don’t use it?**
Yes—YellowBook is designed to stand alone as a private notebook.
